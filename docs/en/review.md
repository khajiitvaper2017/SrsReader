# Review & saved words

Open **Review**, choose **Word** or **In context**, then press **Show answer** or **Space**. Grade with **Again** (**1**) or **Good** (**3**). **Undo last review** restores the previous state unless the word has changed since that review.

Under **Settings → Spaced repetition**:

- **Use only Again and Good:** enabled by default. Turn it off to include **Hard** and **Easy**, using keys **1–4**.
- **New words per day:** default 20; accepts 0–10,000. Due reviews are unlimited. You can still grade words while reading after reaching this limit.
- **Desired retention:** default 90%; accepts 70–99%. Higher values usually mean more reviews.
- **Default card front:** show the word alone or in its sentence.
- **Show sentence translations:** enabled by default; translations appear after revealing the answer.

## Manage words

**Words** lets you search, edit definitions and sentences, or delete a word and its history.

**Master** excludes a word from SRS while preserving its history and examples. **Unmaster** restores its schedule, or introduces it as a new card if it has never been studied. **Suspend** pauses review until **Resume**.

**Refresh from dictionary** replaces saved definitions with current dictionary entries. Updating a dictionary does not automatically replace definitions on your cards.
